Engineered for Everyday Cooking

SmartChef is designed around practical kitchen needs.

🥗

Zero Food Waste

Instead of buying new groceries for every meal, SmartChef prioritizes recipes that use up what you already have on hand. It ranks dishes by match percentage so you know exactly which recipe requires the fewest extra ingredients.

⏱️

Time-Budget Filters

Only have 15 or 20 minutes between classes or after work? Use the cooking time slider to instantly filter for quick stir-fries, pastas, or salads that fit your schedule without sacrificing flavor.

🌱

Strict Dietary Rules

Toggle between Vegetarian and Non-Vegetarian modes with confidence. The filter ensures zero contamination of non-veg recipes when you are cooking vegetarian meals.

💡

Intelligent Ingredient Swaps

Culinary expert rules automatically recognize common pantry substitutes:
• Olive Oil or Cooking Oil in place of Butter / Ghee
• Tofu in place of Paneer
• Milk / Yogurt in place of Heavy Cream

Behind the clean user interface, SmartChef implements symbolic list processing in Common Lisp (SBCL) and Python:

  • Knowledge Base: Stored as nested symbolic lists (name (ingredients...) diet time cuisine) in lisp/recipes.lisp.
  • Recursive Primitives: Uses CAR (head), CDR (tail), CONS (constructor), and MEMBER for structural induction without imperative loops.
  • Set Difference: Missing ingredients are computed via recursive relative complement \(R \setminus U\).
  • Higher Order Functions: REMOVE-IF-NOT filters constraints, and SORT orders results descending by match percentage.
